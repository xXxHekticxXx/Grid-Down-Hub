## When this applies
Most of the year in Central Florida, worst at dawn and dusk and after rain.

## Do this
1. **Clothes:** treat pants, shirts, socks, the hammock and tent with permethrin (follow the label; lasts several washes).
2. **Skin:** an EPA-registered repellent (CDC): DEET (20 to 30%), picaridin (20%), IR3535 or oil of lemon eucalyptus.
3. **Cover up:** long sleeves and pants, tuck pants into socks in tick and chigger country (grass, palmetto).
4. **Nets:** a head net for no-see-ums and mosquitoes, a net over where you sleep.
5. **Camp:** on breezy ground, away from standing water; smoke from the fire helps.
6. **Field trick:** crushed American beautyberry leaves repelled mosquitoes in USDA lab studies; see [Beautyberry](#/a/beautyberry). Use it as a backup only.
7. **Chiggers:** shower or wash with soap soon after walking through grass; itching welts appear hours later around the waist and ankles.

## Warning signs: get help
- Fever, rash or headache in the weeks after many bites or a tick: see a doctor.

## Do NOT
- Do not apply permethrin to skin.
- Do not use repellent under clothing.

## What you need
- Permethrin spray, DEET or picaridin, head net, bed net, long clothing.
