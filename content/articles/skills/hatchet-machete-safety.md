## When this applies
Splitting firewood, clearing palmetto and brush, building shelters, processing a palm heart.

## Do this
1. **Clear your swing:** an arm-plus-blade length all around and overhead; nobody within that circle.
2. **Hatchet splitting:** set the wood on a chopping block, not the ground. Kneel or stand so a miss hits the block, not your leg. For small pieces, rest the blade on the wood and strike the wood and blade together on the block, or baton with a stick.
3. **Machete:** swing away from your body and legs, with the cut angled downward; let the weight do the work. Gloves help grip when sweaty.
4. **Keep them sharp:** a mill file for the hatchet and machete, then a puck stone. Dull blades glance off and cut you.
5. **Sheath** blades whenever you stop, and carry them sheathed at your side, blade down.
6. **Rest:** most cuts happen when you're tired, hot or in a hurry.
7. **Eye protection** for splitting (chips fly).

## Warning signs: get help
- A cut that spurts or soaks: [Severe bleeding](#/a/severe-bleeding). Keep the tourniquet within reach when chopping.

## Do NOT
- Do not chop toward yourself or with your leg in line with the swing.
- Do not leave blades lying in grass.
- Do not baton a machete through big wood; it bends.

## What you need
- Hatchet, machete, sheaths, a file and puck stone, gloves, safety glasses, a chopping block.
