## When this applies
You need more cord than you have, or thin cord for repairs.

## Do this
1. **Paracord (550 cord):** cut a length, pull out the inner strands (7 in true 550 cord). Each strand holds tens of pounds; use them for sewing, fishing line, lashings. The sheath alone is still a strong cord.
2. **Plant fiber:** cabbage palm leaf fibers, yucca or Spanish bayonet leaves (soak and scrape to fibers), inner bark of basswood or hickory, cattail leaves (weak, for light work).
3. **Reverse wrap (two-ply):** take a bundle of fibers, fold it with one side longer. Pinch at the fold; twist the top strand away from you, then cross it toward you over the bottom strand. Repeat, swapping. The twists lock together. Splice in new fibers before one side runs out.
4. **Lashings:** use square lashing to join poles at right angles for shelters and racks.

## Warning signs: get help
- None specific; never trust plant cord for climbing or rescue.

## Do NOT
- Do not cut live yucca or palm on public land; use dead material.

## What you need
- Paracord, a knife, gloves.
