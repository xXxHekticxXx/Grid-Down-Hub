## When this applies
Every night in the field, especially bugging out to the Ocala NF.

## Do this
1. **Arrive with daylight left** (two hours) to choose and set up.
2. **High and dry:** slight rise, sandy soil drains best; avoid low spots, dry ditches and lake edges that flood in a downpour.
3. **Look up:** no dead trees or hanging limbs. Pines snap in wind.
4. **Water close but not too close:** 100+ feet from springs, lakes and streams (Forest Service rule; also gators and mosquitoes).
5. **Use a previously used site** (Forest Service asks this) and keep it low-visibility from roads.
6. **Truck facing out,** clear path to leave fast; know two ways out on the forest roads.
7. **Check the ground** for fire ant mounds, animal trails and holes (gopher tortoise burrows are protected; don't block them).
8. **Gun season:** camping is allowed only at designated campsites during general gun season in the Ocala WMA (Forest Service).

## Warning signs: get help
- Storms building: identify where you'll ride it out (the truck for lightning) before it hits.

## Do NOT
- Do not camp under dead trees or in low ground.
- Do not camp near developed recreation areas when dispersed camping (not allowed there).

## What you need
- The map with Campgrounds layer, a shovel, a tarp.
