## When this applies
Fuel for the stove is short, or you're in camp with a fire allowed.

## Do this
1. **Burn down to coals** (30 to 45 minutes); flames blacken pots and burn food.
2. **Grate** on rocks or logs over the coals, or a tripod of green-free dead poles with a pot hung by wire or chain.
3. **Foil packets:** meat, potatoes, onions and seasoning sealed in double foil, in the coals 15 to 30 minutes, turned once.
4. **Pot with lid** boils water much faster and uses less wood.
5. **Dutch oven:** coals under and on the lid bake bread and stews.
6. **Fish:** on a green-stick split skewer or in foil; cook to 145 °F ([Safe cooking temperatures](#/a/safe-cooking-temps)).
7. **Clean up:** burn off food bits, pack out trash, drown the fire.

## Warning signs: get help
- Burns: [Burns](#/a/burns).

## Do NOT
- Do not use rocks from rivers or springs in a fire ring; trapped water can make them explode.
- Do not cook in the tent or under the tarp.

## What you need
- A grate, foil, a lidded pot, a cast iron skillet or Dutch oven, leather gloves, tongs.
