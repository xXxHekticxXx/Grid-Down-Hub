## When this applies
Keeping the knife, hatchet and machete working. A sharp tool is a safe tool.

## Do this
1. **Angle:** about 20° per side for a knife, 25 to 30° for hatchets and machetes (thicker, tougher edge).
2. **Coarse to fine:** a file for nicks on axes and machetes; a coarse then fine stone for knives.
3. **Raise a burr:** stroke one side until you can feel a slight wire edge along the whole other side with your thumb (across, not along, the edge).
4. **Switch sides** and repeat with fewer strokes, then alternate single strokes.
5. **Strop** on a leather belt or the back of a cardboard box, edge trailing.
6. **Field sharpening:** the unglazed bottom ring of a ceramic mug or a fine-grained river stone works in a pinch.
7. **Oil** blades after use in humid Florida air to prevent rust.

## Warning signs: get help
- Cuts while sharpening: pressure and a bandage; see [Cuts and infection](#/a/wounds-infection).

## Do NOT
- Do not sharpen toward a hand holding the blade's edge side.

## What you need
- A mill file, a combination whetstone, a strop, oil.
