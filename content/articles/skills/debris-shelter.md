## When this applies
No tarp or tent, and you need shelter for the night from rain, wind or cold.

## Do this
1. **Debris hut:** prop a long ridgepole (9 to 10 feet) on a stump or forked stick at about hip height, the other end on the ground. Lean sticks (ribs) along both sides close together. Pile leaves, pine straw or grass over it at least 2 to 3 feet thick. Fill the inside with dry leaves for bedding. Crawl in feet first; plug the door with a pile of debris.
2. **Palm-frond lean-to:** a horizontal pole between two trees, poles leaning against it, and saw palmetto or cabbage palm fronds layered from the bottom up like shingles so rain runs off.
3. **Raised bed:** in wet ground, a platform of poles laid across two logs, covered with fronds, keeps you off the wet and the ants.
4. **Size small:** your body heats a small space.

## Warning signs: get help
- Shivering in a wet shelter: see [Hypothermia](#/a/hypothermia).

## Do NOT
- Do not gather debris piles with bare hands without checking for snakes, spiders and fire ants.
- Do not cut live vegetation where it's prohibited; on public land use downed material.

## What you need
- A hatchet or machete, gloves, an hour of daylight.
