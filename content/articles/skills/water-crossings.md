## When this applies
A flooded road, a creek or a marsh between you and where you need to be.

## Do this
1. **First, look for another way:** a bridge, the long way around, or wait for water to drop.
2. **Pick the spot:** wide, shallow, slow water with a firm bottom, visible exit on the far bank.
3. **Unbuckle the pack's hip belt and chest strap** so you can shed it if you fall.
4. **Keep boots on** to protect your feet.
5. **Face upstream,** shuffle sideways, test each step, use a sturdy pole as a third leg upstream of you.
6. **Groups:** link arms and cross together.
7. **If swept away:** float on your back feet-first downstream, steer toward the bank.

## Warning signs: get help
- Water above the knee and moving fast: don't cross.
- Murky slow water in Florida can hide alligators and cottonmouths; avoid wading at dawn and dusk.

## Do NOT
- Do not walk or drive through floodwater (Ready.gov): 6 inches of moving water can knock you down.
- Do not cross tied to a rope fixed on both banks.

## What you need
- A walking pole, a dry bag for the phone, dry socks after.
