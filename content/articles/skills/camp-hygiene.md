## When this applies
Living outside for days or weeks.

## Do this
1. **Hands:** wash with soap and treated water before cooking and eating and after the toilet. Sanitizer as backup.
2. **Water:** treat all of it ([Making water safe](#/a/water-treatment)).
3. **Food:** cooler at 40 °F or below with ice; eat perishables first; clean dishes with hot water and a bleach rinse.
4. **Toilet:** cat holes 6 inches deep, 100+ feet from water, trails and camp (Forest Service). Designate one direction from camp.
5. **Body:** wash face, armpits, groin and feet daily; dry socks; clean clothes when you can. Chafing and fungal rashes in Florida heat get infected.
6. **Teeth:** brush with treated water.
7. **Ticks and chiggers:** check every evening; permethrin on clothing.
8. **Trash:** pack it in, pack it out; lock up food from bears and raccoons.

## Warning signs: get help
- Diarrhea or fever in camp: [Diarrhea and oral rehydration](#/a/diarrhea-ors).

## Do NOT
- Do not wash with soap directly in springs or lakes.

## What you need
- Soap, sanitizer, a trowel, toilet paper in a zip bag, a basin, a cooler, permethrin.
