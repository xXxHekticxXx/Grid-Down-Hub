## When this applies
Every night outside, in the truck or the camper without power.

## Do this
1. **Insulate from the ground:** a sleeping pad (foam doesn't pop) or a cot. The ground steals heat even in Florida in winter.
2. **Summer:** a sheet or light blanket, a hammock with a bug net (air underneath cools you), a battery fan; wet a cloth for your neck.
3. **Cold fronts** (lows in the 30s and 40s happen December to February): a sleeping bag rated to 30 to 40 °F, a wool hat, dry socks only for sleeping.
4. **Stay dry:** change out of sweaty clothes before bed.
5. **Truck sleeping:** crack windows for air, with bug screens; never with the engine running ([Carbon monoxide](#/a/carbon-monoxide-poisoning)).
6. **Watch rotation** if security matters: shifts of 2 to 3 hours so everyone gets a block of sleep.

## Warning signs: get help
- Several nights without sleep: judgment and reflexes fall like being drunk. Prioritize rest.

## Do NOT
- Do not sleep in the truck with the engine running or a stove inside.

## What you need
- Sleeping pad, sleeping bag or blanket, hammock with net, earplugs.
