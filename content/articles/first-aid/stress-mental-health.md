## When this applies
Days into a disaster, fear, anger, numbness, trouble sleeping, racing thoughts, or crying spells are common. So are irritability and exhaustion from constant vigilance. These are normal reactions to abnormal events.

## Do this
1. **Basics first:** water, food, sleep (even short naps), a cool place to rest.
2. **Keep a routine:** set times for meals, chores, radio checks, sleep. It gives the day shape.
3. **Limit news and rumors** to scheduled checks; rumors spread fast when power is out.
4. **Slow breathing for panic:** breathe in through the nose for 4 counts, hold for 4, out for 6 to 8. Repeat for two minutes.
5. **Stay connected:** check on neighbors; talk with someone you trust.
6. **Take care of the dog together;** animals steady people.
7. **Call or text the Disaster Distress Helpline, 1-800-985-5990,** any time, free, for crisis counseling about disasters. No name needed.

## Warning signs: get help
- **Thoughts of suicide or harming someone: call or text 988 now,** or 911 if in immediate danger.
- Can't function, can't sleep for days, heavy drinking or drug use, or hearing or seeing things others don't.

## Do NOT
- Do not use alcohol to sleep; it worsens sleep and judgment.
- Do not make big, irreversible decisions while exhausted if they can wait.

## What you need
- 988 and 1-800-985-5990 are saved in Home → Emergency numbers.
