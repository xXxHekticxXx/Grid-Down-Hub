## When this applies
Headache, dizziness, weakness, upset stomach, vomiting, chest pain or confusion, often in several people (and pets) at once, often worse in the camper or truck and better outside. People asleep or drinking can die before they wake. Generators, charcoal grills, camp stoves, propane heaters and running engines all make CO.

## Do this
1. **Get everyone, including the dog, outside into fresh air right away.** Don't stop to open windows.
2. **Call 911.** Tell them "carbon monoxide."
3. **Turn off the source only if you can do it from outside without going back in.**
4. **Anyone not breathing normally: [CPR](#/a/cpr).**
5. **Don't go back in** until the fire department says it's safe or the CO alarm reads clear with doors open.

## Warning signs: get help
- Anyone confused, unconscious, pregnant, a child, or with chest pain: hospital now (they may need oxygen treatment).
- Symptoms that came back each time you went inside.

## Do NOT
- Do not run a generator in the camper, a garage, a shed or under an awning, even with doors open. Keep it more than 20 feet from doors, windows and vents with the exhaust pointed away.
- Do not use a charcoal grill, camp stove or gas oven to heat the camper.
- Do not sleep in a running vehicle.

## What you need
- A battery CO alarm in the camper near where you sleep, and one in the truck cab if you'll sleep there. Replace per the label (about every 5 years).
- See [Generator and CO safety](#/a/generator-co-safety).
