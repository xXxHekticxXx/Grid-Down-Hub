## When this applies
Walking out with a pack, long days in wet boots after flooding, or new boots. Wet feet for days can lead to immersion foot (white, wrinkled, numb, painful).

## Do this
1. **Stop at the first hot spot.** Cover it with moleskin, athletic tape or a blister bandage before it becomes a blister.
2. **Small intact blister:** pad around it (a donut of moleskin) and leave it intact.
3. **Large, tense blister that will burst anyway:** clean it, drain it with a sterilized needle at the edge, leave the roof of skin on, cover with ointment and a bandage.
4. **Torn blister:** clean, ointment, non-stick dressing, tape.
5. **Keep feet dry:** change socks at least daily, more when wet; dry feet and socks at every rest stop; sleep with dry feet.
6. **Air the feet** and massage them at night. Trim toenails straight across.

## Warning signs: get help
- Spreading redness, pus, red streaks or fever: infection; see [Cuts, scrapes and preventing infection](#/a/wounds-infection).
- Feet that stay white, numb, swollen or very painful after being wet for days.

## Do NOT
- Do not keep walking on a hot spot "to finish the day."
- Do not peel off the blister roof.

## What you need
- Moleskin, athletic tape, blister bandages, a sewing needle and lighter, at least three pairs of wool or synthetic socks, foot powder.
