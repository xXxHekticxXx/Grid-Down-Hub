## When this applies
Dust, sand, sawdust or an eyelash in the eye; or a hit from a branch, a chip from a grinder, chainsaw or hammer, or a fish hook.

## Do this
1. **Don't rub it.**
2. **Small, loose particles:** blink and let tears wash it, or rinse with clean tap water or eyewash, head turned so the water runs away from the other eye.
3. **Contact lens:** take it out and stop wearing contacts until it feels normal.
4. **High-speed or sharp injury, or anything stuck in the eye:** don't try to remove it. **Tape a paper or plastic cup or a hard eye shield over the eye** to keep anything from pressing on it, and get to a doctor.
5. **Afterward:** acetaminophen or ibuprofen for soreness.

## Warning signs: get help
- Injury from grinding, hammering metal, a chainsaw or machinery; a cut on the eye; bleeding in the eye; an irregular pupil; loss of vision: **immediate medical care.**
- A gritty feeling that doesn't go away after rinsing.

## Do NOT
- Do not rub the eye or press on it.
- Do not try to pull out something stuck in the eye.
- Do not use tweezers near the eye.

## What you need
- Safety glasses for chainsaw and hatchet work. Eyewash or saline, a plastic cup and tape.
