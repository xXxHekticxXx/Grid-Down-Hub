## When this applies
Someone struck by lightning, shocked by a downed power line, a wet generator, an extension cord, or a house wired backward to a generator.

## Do this
1. **Electrical shock: don't touch them until the power is off.** Shut off the generator or main breaker, or wait for the utility. Stay at least 35 feet from downed lines; the ground around them can be live.
2. **Lightning:** people who were struck carry no charge and are **safe to touch right away.** Move them to safety if the storm is still active.
3. **Call 911.**
4. **Not breathing normally: start [CPR](#/a/cpr)** and use an AED. Lightning victims often recover with fast CPR.
5. **Several people struck:** help the ones who look dead first; those who are moving and talking will probably survive.
6. **Then:** cover burns with clean dry dressings ([Burns](#/a/burns)) and check for broken bones from being thrown.

## Warning signs: get help
- Everyone who was struck by lightning or had a significant electrical shock needs to be checked at a hospital, even if they seem fine. Heart rhythm problems can show up later.

## Do NOT
- Do not touch a person in contact with a live wire, or use a wet stick to move a wire.
- Do not drive over downed lines.
- Do not wait out a thunderstorm under a tree; see [Lightning](#/a/lightning).

## What you need
- Know where the camper's main breaker and the generator's off switch are.
