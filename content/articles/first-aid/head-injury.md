## When this applies
A blow or jolt to the head: a fall from a ladder or the camper roof, a tree limb, a vehicle crash. Concussion signs: headache, nausea, balance problems, trouble concentrating, confusion, irritability, unusual tiredness.

## Do this
1. **Stop the activity right away.** No more ladders, chainsaws or driving that day.
2. **Cuts on the scalp bleed a lot:** firm pressure with gauze. Gentle if the skull might be broken.
3. **If they might also have a neck injury,** keep them still: see [Spine and neck injury](#/a/spinal-injury).
4. **Watch them closely for the next 24 hours.** Wake them a few times the first night and check they know where they are and can talk normally.
5. **Rest.** Acetaminophen (Tylenol) for headache.

## Warning signs: get help
Call 911 if they:
- Were knocked out, even briefly.
- Have a headache that gets worse, vomit repeatedly, are confused, very sleepy or hard to wake, or have a seizure.
- Have vision changes, weakness or numbness, slurred speech, unequal pupils.
- Have clear fluid from the nose or ears, a dent or swelling in the skull, or bruising behind the ears or around both eyes.
- Take blood thinners.

## Do NOT
- Do not let them return to work or play the same day.
- Do not give alcohol or sleeping pills.
- Do not move someone with neck pain unless they're in danger.

## What you need
- Gauze, a cold pack, acetaminophen.
