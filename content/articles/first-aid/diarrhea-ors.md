## When this applies
Diarrhea or vomiting from contaminated water or food, spoiled food after a power outage, or a stomach bug. The danger is dehydration, especially for children, older adults and in heat.

## Do this
1. **Make oral rehydration solution (ORS):** a store packet in safe water as directed, or the WHO home recipe: **6 level teaspoons of sugar plus ½ level teaspoon of salt in 1 liter (about 1 quart) of safe drinking water.** Stir until dissolved.
2. **Drink small sips often:** a few swallows every few minutes, even while vomiting. Aim for a cup after each loose stool, more in heat.
3. **Keep eating** simple foods when you can (rice, crackers, bananas, broth).
4. **Wash hands with soap** after the toilet and before handling food or water; keep the sick person's dishes and towels separate.
5. **Use only safe water** for drinking, ice and brushing teeth: [Making water safe](#/a/water-treatment).

## Warning signs: get help
- Can't keep any fluids down, very little or no urine, very dark urine, dizziness when standing, confusion, sunken eyes.
- Blood in the stool, high fever, severe belly pain, or diarrhea lasting more than a few days.
- Babies, small children and older adults dehydrate fast: get care early.

## Do NOT
- Do not make ORS too salty: measure. Too much salt makes it worse.
- Do not use anti-diarrhea medicine if there is fever or blood in the stool.
- Do not rely on sugary soda or juice alone; they lack salt and can worsen diarrhea.

## What you need
- ORS packets in every kit (cheap at pharmacies), measuring spoons, a 1-liter bottle marked at the fill line.
