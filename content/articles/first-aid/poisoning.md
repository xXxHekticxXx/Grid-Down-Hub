## When this applies
Someone swallowed, breathed in, or got on their skin or in their eyes something harmful: medicines, bleach, fuel, lamp oil, pesticides, plants, mushrooms, or a child got into something.

## Do this
1. **Not breathing or won't wake up: call 911** and start [CPR](#/a/cpr).
2. **Call Poison Help: 1-800-222-1222.** Free, 24/7. Call even before symptoms show. Have the container in your hand.
3. **Breathed fumes:** get them to fresh air right away. For generator or stove fumes see [Carbon monoxide poisoning](#/a/carbon-monoxide-poisoning).
4. **On the skin:** take off contaminated clothing; rinse with running water for 15 to 20 minutes.
5. **In the eyes:** rinse with running water for 15 to 20 minutes; see [Chemical in the eye](#/a/chemical-exposure).
6. **Tell Poison Help:** age and weight, what it was, how much, how (swallowed, breathed, skin), when, what you've already done, and how far you are from a hospital.
7. Follow what they say.

## Warning signs: get help
- Trouble breathing, seizure, drowsiness, burns in the mouth, repeated vomiting: 911.
- Swallowed button batteries, magnets, lamp oil, gasoline or kerosene: emergency even if they seem fine.

## Do NOT
- Do not make them vomit.
- Do not give activated charcoal unless Poison Help tells you to.
- Do not give milk, salt water or "antidotes" from the internet.
- Do not siphon gasoline by mouth.

## What you need
- The Poison Help number (saved in Home → Emergency numbers).
- Keep medicines and chemicals in original labeled containers, high up.
