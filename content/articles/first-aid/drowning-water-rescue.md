## When this applies
Someone struggling in a lake, spring, river or floodwater, or pulled from the water not breathing normally. Drowning is often silent: no waving or yelling, head low in the water.

## Do this
1. **Call 911** or have someone call.
2. **Reach, throw, don't go:** reach with a pole, branch or paddle while you lie flat; throw anything that floats (a cooler, a life jacket, a jug) with a rope if you have one. Go in only if trained, with a flotation device.
3. **Out of the water and not breathing normally: start [CPR](#/a/cpr) with breaths right away.** Breaths matter most in drowning because the cause is lack of oxygen.
4. **Breathing:** roll them on their side, keep them warm, stay with them.
5. **Possible diving injury in shallow water:** float them out keeping the head and body in line, then follow [Spine and neck injury](#/a/spinal-injury).

## Warning signs: get help
- Anyone pulled from the water who was unresponsive, needed breaths, or has coughing, short breath or confusion afterward needs a hospital. Lungs can worsen hours later.

## Do NOT
- Do not jump in after someone without something that floats; panicked swimmers pull rescuers under.
- Do not try to press water out of the stomach or lungs.
- Do not walk or drive into floodwater: 6 inches of moving water can knock you down and 1 foot can float a vehicle.

## What you need
- A throw rope or a floating cooler and life jackets when camping by springs and lakes; alligators are also in every Florida water body: see [Alligators](#/a/alligators).
