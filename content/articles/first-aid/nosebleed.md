## When this applies
Bleeding from the nose, from dry air, picking, a blow to the face, or blood thinners.

## Do this
1. **Sit down and lean slightly forward.**
2. **Pinch the soft lower part of the nose shut** with thumb and finger, firmly, **for 10 to 15 minutes without letting go to check.**
3. **Breathe through the mouth and spit out blood.**
4. After it stops, avoid blowing the nose or heavy lifting for a day.

::timer 15 Pinch the nose; don't let go to check

## Warning signs: get help
- Still bleeding after 15 minutes of continuous pressure, or light-headed.
- After a blow to the face: signs of [head injury](#/a/head-injury), a crooked nose or facial fracture.
- On blood thinners or with a bleeding disorder and it doesn't stop.

## Do NOT
- Do not tilt the head back or lie down; blood runs down the throat.
- Do not pack the nose with tissues and stop pinching.

## What you need
- Tissues or gauze, a timer.
