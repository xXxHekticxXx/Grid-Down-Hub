## When this applies
Sepsis is the body's extreme reaction to an infection. It can start from an infected cut, a bite, a urinary infection, pneumonia or a stomach infection. After disasters, with dirty water and slow medical care, it is a real danger.

## Do this
1. **Know the signs (CDC):** one or more of confusion or disorientation; fever, shivering or feeling very cold; fast heart rate or weak pulse; shortness of breath; extreme pain or discomfort; clammy or sweaty skin.
2. **Any of these in someone with a known or likely infection: call 911 or get to an ER now.** Say "I'm worried about sepsis."
3. **While waiting:** keep them comfortable, give fluids if they're alert and can swallow, and note when symptoms started and what infection they had.

## Warning signs: get help
- Sepsis is always an emergency; it can kill within hours and needs IV antibiotics.

## Do NOT
- Do not wait for a wound infection to "come to a head."
- Do not treat it with leftover antibiotics at home in place of getting care.

## What you need
- A thermometer, and the habit of checking every wound daily: see [Cuts, scrapes and preventing infection](#/a/wounds-infection).
