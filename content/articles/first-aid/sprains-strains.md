## When this applies
Pain, swelling and limited movement after a twist, fall or lifting. A sprain is a ligament (ankle, knee, wrist); a strain is a muscle or tendon (back, hamstring).

## Do this
1. **Rest it.** Avoid what hurts.
2. **Cold:** a bag of ice and water wrapped in a damp cloth, **20 to 30 minutes at a time,** 3 to 4 times a day. Never ice straight on skin.
3. **Compression wrap** on an ankle can make it more comfortable. Wrap from the toes up, snug not tight; check toes stay pink and warm.
4. **Raise it** above the heart when resting.
5. **Over-the-counter pain medicine** (acetaminophen or ibuprofen) as the label says.
6. **On the move with no choice:** tape or wrap the ankle firmly over the boot, use a walking stick, and reduce the load.

## Warning signs: get help
- Can't take 4 steps, can't move the joint, or the bone itself is tender: possible fracture; see [Broken bones](#/a/fractures-splinting).
- Numbness, a cold or pale foot or hand, or the wrap feels too tight.
- Not improving after a few days.

## Do NOT
- Do not put ice directly on skin or leave it longer than 30 minutes.
- Do not wrap so tightly it cuts off circulation or leave a wrap on overnight without checking.

## What you need
- Elastic (ACE) wraps, instant cold packs or a cooler with ice, a walking stick.
