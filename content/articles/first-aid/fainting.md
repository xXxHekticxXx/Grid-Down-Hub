## When this applies
Feeling light-headed, sweaty, pale, tunnel vision, nausea, "about to pass out," or someone who briefly passed out and came back quickly. Common causes: standing too long in heat, dehydration, the sight of blood, pain, getting up fast.

## Do this
1. **Sit or lie down immediately** so they don't fall. Lying flat with legs raised helps.
2. **Physical counter-pressure (AHA/Red Cross):** if they feel it coming, have them squat, cross their legs and squeeze the thighs together, or clench their fists and tense their arms. This can stop the faint.
3. **Cool them, loosen clothing, give water** once fully awake.
4. **Let them rest 10 to 15 minutes** before getting up slowly: sit first, then stand.

## Warning signs: get help
Call 911 or get to an ER if they:
- Fainted during exercise or work, or while lying down.
- Had chest pain, palpitations, short breath or a bad headache.
- Are pregnant, older, have heart disease, or don't wake up within a minute.
- Hit their head, or have any [stroke](#/a/stroke) signs.

## Do NOT
- Do not prop someone up who is fainting; let them lie down.
- Do not splash or slap them.

## What you need
- Water and shade.
