## When this applies
Florida has black widows and brown widows (cramping pain, sweating, muscle rigidity) and occasional recluse-type spiders (painful sore that can ulcer over days), and small scorpions whose stings hurt but are rarely dangerous here. Woodpiles, sheds, boots and gear left outside are common hiding spots.

## Do this
1. **Wash the area** with soap and water.
2. **Cold pack or ice wrapped in cloth** for pain and swelling.
3. **Over-the-counter pain reliever** (acetaminophen or ibuprofen); for scorpion stings, lidocaine cream on unbroken skin also helps.
4. **Watch it** over the next days; mark the edge of redness with a pen.

## Warning signs: get help
- **Call 911 for trouble breathing, severe muscle cramps or rigidity, dizziness or confusion.**
- See a doctor if pain spreads beyond the bite or becomes severe and isn't controlled by OTC medicine, or an open wound develops.
- Signs of [anaphylaxis](#/a/anaphylaxis).

## Do NOT
- Do not cut or suck the bite.
- Do not reach into woodpiles or put on boots without checking and shaking them first.

## What you need
- Cold packs, pain reliever, lidocaine cream, gloves for moving wood.
