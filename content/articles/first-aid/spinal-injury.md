## When this applies
A fall from height (roof, tree stand, ladder), a vehicle crash or rollover, diving into shallow water, or a heavy object falling on someone, with neck or back pain, numbness, tingling or weakness.

## Do this
1. **Tell them not to move.** Ask them to stay as still as possible.
2. **Call 911.**
3. **Leave them where they are** unless the place is dangerous (fire, traffic, rising water, CO). If you must move them, keep the head, neck and body in a line and move them together, with help.
4. **Keep them warm and calm;** check breathing.
5. **If they vomit,** roll them onto their side keeping head and body together.
6. Not breathing normally: [CPR](#/a/cpr) takes priority over the spine.

## Warning signs: get help
- Numbness, weakness, or loss of feeling or movement in arms or legs.
- Loss of bladder or bowel control.
- Severe neck or back pain after an accident.

## Do NOT
- Do not use a rigid collar or board as a layperson; current guidelines advise against it.
- Do not immobilize a person with a gunshot or stab wound "to protect the spine"; it costs time and lives.
- Do not have them "try to walk it off."

## What you need
- A blanket, help from others if moving is unavoidable.
