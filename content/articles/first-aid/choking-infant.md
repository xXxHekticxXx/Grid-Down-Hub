## When this applies
A baby under 1 year can't cry, cough or breathe, or makes high-pitched noises or no sound, and may turn blue.

## Do this
1. **Have someone call 911.**
2. **Back blows:** lay the baby face down along your forearm, head lower than the chest, supporting the jaw with your hand (don't cover the mouth). Rest your arm on your thigh. Give **5 firm back blows** between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand.
3. **Chest thrusts:** turn the baby face up on your other forearm, head still lower. Give **5 chest thrusts with the heel of one hand** on the center of the chest just below the nipple line, about 1½ inches deep (AHA 2025).
4. **Keep alternating 5 and 5** until the object comes out or the baby becomes unresponsive.
5. **Baby goes limp:** start infant [CPR](#/a/cpr). Look in the mouth before breaths and remove the object only if you can see it.

## Warning signs: get help
- Every infant who choked badly enough to need back blows should see a doctor.
- Ongoing cough, wheeze, drooling, or trouble feeding.

## Do NOT
- Do not give abdominal thrusts (Heimlich) to a baby.
- Do not hold the baby upside down by the feet or shake them.
- Do not sweep the mouth blindly.

## What you need
- Your hands.
