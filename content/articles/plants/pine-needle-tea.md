## When this applies
A long stretch without fresh fruit or vegetables. Pines are everywhere in the forest.

## Do this
1. **Identify true pines:** needles in bundles (2 or 3 for slash pine, 3 long needles for longleaf), not flat single needles (yews and other conifers).
2. **Pick fresh green needles,** rinse in treated water and chop.
3. **Steep a handful in hot (not boiling) water** for 10 to 20 minutes. Boiling destroys more vitamin C.
4. Drink a cup a day as a supplement, not a meal.

## Warning signs: get help
- Pregnant women should avoid pine needle tea; some pine species have caused miscarriage in cattle.

## Do NOT
- Do not use yew (flat needles, red berries): deadly. Florida yew is rare, but ornamental yews exist.
- Do not use needles from Norfolk Island pine or other ornamentals, or from trees sprayed with chemicals.

## What you need
- A pot and fire, a cup.
