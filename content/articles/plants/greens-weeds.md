## When this applies
These grow in lawns, gardens and roadsides right around the camper, often year-round.

## Do this
1. **Purslane:** low, sprawling, reddish stems with fat, smooth, paddle-shaped leaves, tiny yellow flowers. Crunchy and lemony, raw or cooked.
2. **Spanish needles (Bidens alba):** white daisy-like flowers with yellow centers and black needle seeds that stick to clothes. Young leaves cooked as greens; flowers raw.
3. **Dollarweed (pennywort):** round, shiny leaves the size of a coin on a stem attached at the center (like an umbrella). Leaves raw or cooked, peppery.
4. **Wood sorrel (Oxalis):** clover-like heart-shaped leaflets in 3s, yellow or pink flowers, sour taste. Small amounts raw.
5. **Florida betony:** square stems, opposite leaves, and white segmented tubers that look like rattlesnake rattles; tubers crisp and edible raw (UF/IFAS lists it).
6. **Wash everything** in treated water; cook greens if you aren't sure the area is clean.

## Warning signs: get help
- Stomach upset is usually from eating too much. Stop and drink water.

## Do NOT
- **Spurge** looks like purslane but is thin, flat and bleeds milky sap when broken. Purslane has no milky sap. Never eat a plant with milky sap.
- Do not eat large amounts of wood sorrel (oxalic acid), especially with kidney problems.
- Do not gather where lawns were sprayed or along busy roads.

## What you need
- A knife, a bag, treated water to wash.
