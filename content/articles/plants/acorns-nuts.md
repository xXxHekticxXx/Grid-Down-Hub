## When this applies
Live oaks, laurel oaks, turkey oaks and water oaks drop acorns in the fall; hickories and wild pecans grow in hammocks. Nuts are the richest calorie source you can gather.

## Do this
1. **Gather** sound acorns without holes (weevils); float them in water and discard ones that float.
2. **Shell** them and crush or chop the nutmeats.
3. **Leach the tannins:** hot method: boil in water, pour off the brown water, add fresh boiling water, repeat until no longer bitter (several changes). Cold method: soak chopped acorns in cold water changed twice a day for several days, or in a cloth bag in a clean flowing stream.
4. **Dry and roast,** or grind into a meal for flatbread or porridge.
5. **Hickory nuts** need no leaching: crack and pick out the meat. Pecans the same.

## Warning signs: get help
- Stomach upset or constipation means they weren't leached enough.

## Do NOT
- Do not eat unleached acorns in quantity: tannins upset the stomach and can harm the kidneys.
- Do not eat moldy nuts.

## What you need
- A pot, water, a hatchet or rock to crack shells, a cloth bag.
