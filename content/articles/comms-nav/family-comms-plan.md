## When this applies
Before anything happens. When phones go down, people who planned find each other.

## Do this
1. **Contacts:** the people you'd check on and who'd check on you; their phone numbers written down (not only in the phone). My Plan → Contacts.
2. **Out-of-area contact:** someone outside Florida everyone texts to say "I'm OK." Long-distance texts often work when local lines are jammed (Ready.gov).
3. **Meeting places:** one near home (the end of the driveway, a neighbor's house), one in town (a church or store), and your bug-out destination. Save them as Meeting point pins on the map.
4. **Radio plan:** FRS/GMRS channel, backup channel, check-in times; ham repeater if licensed. My Plan → Radio.
5. **Texts beat calls;** keep messages short with location and status.
6. **Leave a note** at the camper when you leave: where you went, when, the route (in a sealed bag, inside where only friends will look).
7. **Print the plan** (My Plan → Print) and keep a copy in the truck.

## Warning signs: get help
- Someone doesn't check in at the agreed time: start your planned search (last known location, route).

## Do NOT
- Do not rely only on phone contacts; batteries die.

## What you need
- My Plan filled in, printed copies, pins on the map for meeting points.
