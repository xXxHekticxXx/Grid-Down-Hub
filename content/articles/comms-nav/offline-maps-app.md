## When this applies
Navigating with no signal: the map packs on this phone hold everything.

## Do this
1. **Install packs first:** Setup → download all, while you have internet. Settings shows the map date and storage used.
2. **Where am I:** tap the location button. The panel shows DD, DDM and USNG coordinates. GPS needs a clear sky view; it can take a minute.
3. **Pins:** long-press (or right-click on the laptop) to drop a pin; name it, set a type (meeting point, cache, camp, water, danger). Pins show distance and bearing from you.
4. **Measure:** the ruler tool measures distance along a path you tap.
5. **Layers:** turn on hospitals, shelters (pet-friendly flagged; confirm open via AlertLake), springs and water (treat before drinking), gas, groceries and more.
6. **Topo overlay:** Layers → USGS topo, with an opacity slider.
7. **Record a track:** start recording before you walk into the forest; it lays a breadcrumb back.
8. **Export:** the PDF button saves the current view as a printable map.
9. **Add your own maps:** Maps → import GPX/KML/GeoJSON routes, PMTiles map files, or photograph a paper map and line it up with two points.

## Warning signs: get help
- GPS position jumping around: wait for more satellites, or move into the open.

## Do NOT
- Do not wait for an outage to install packs.

## What you need
- The map packs installed; a power bank.
