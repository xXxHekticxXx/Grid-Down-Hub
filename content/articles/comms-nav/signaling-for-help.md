## When this applies
Lost, injured, stuck in the forest, or trapped after a storm, and you need searchers or aircraft to find you.

## Do this
1. **Stay put** if people know your plan; you're easier to find in one place.
2. **Whistle:** three sharp blasts, repeated. Carries farther than a voice and uses less energy. Tools → SOS can sound it.
3. **Light at night:** SOS: three short, three long, three short. Tools → SOS flashes it with the phone light.
4. **Signal mirror** (or any shiny surface) by day: aim the flash at aircraft or distant vehicles; a flash can be seen for miles.
5. **Three fires in a triangle** about 100 feet apart, or one fire with green leaves for smoke by day.
6. **Ground signals:** big shapes in contrasting colors in an opening: an X means "need medical help," a V means "need assistance" (ATP 3-50.21 chapter 7 shows others). Make them at least 10 feet long.
7. **Bright gear:** a tarp, a space blanket shiny side up, orange clothing spread out.
8. **Texts:** keep trying 911 texts; they can get through on weak signal.

## Warning signs: get help
- Injured and alone: use your energy on signals, water and shelter, not walking out on a bad leg.

## Do NOT
- Do not start a signal fire in dry, windy conditions you can't control.
- Do not leave a vehicle to walk away unless you're sure of the way and the conditions.

## What you need
- A whistle on every keychain and pack, a signal mirror, a headlamp, a bright tarp or space blanket, this app's SOS tool.
