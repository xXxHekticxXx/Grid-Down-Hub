## When this applies
Telling 911, a rescuer or a neighbor exactly where you are, especially in the forest where there are no addresses.

## Do this
1. **Find your position:** Tools → Compass or the Map's GPS panel shows three formats:
   - **DD** (decimal degrees): 28.80750, -81.56030, what most apps use.
   - **DDM** (degrees, decimal minutes): 28° 48.450′ N, 81° 33.618′ W, used by many GPS units and aviation.
   - **USNG**: for example 17R MM 45325 86787, used by Florida emergency managers and on US Topo sheets.
2. **Reading USNG (FGDC):** zone and band (17R), the 100-km square (two letters), then easting and northing digits. **Read right, then up.** More digits = more precise: 4 digits = 1 km, 6 = 100 m, 8 = 10 m, 10 = 1 m.
3. **Giving a location by voice:** say the format first, then the numbers slowly, and repeat them back.
4. **Paste coordinates** into the Map's search box to jump there and drop a pin.

## Warning signs: get help
- Calling 911 from the woods: give a road name or landmark too, and keep the phone on.

## Do NOT
- Do not mix up formats (DD vs DDM) when reading to someone; it puts them miles off.

## What you need
- This app with a GPS fix (works without signal).
