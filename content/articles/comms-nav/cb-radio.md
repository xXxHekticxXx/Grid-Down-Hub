## When this applies
On the road during an evacuation: truckers on channel 19 report closures, fuel and traffic.

## Do this
1. **Channel 19 (27.185 MHz):** highway traffic and road information.
2. **Channel 9 (27.065 MHz): emergency communications and traveler assistance only** (FCC rule). Use it to call for help.
3. **Any channel** may be used for emergencies, and emergency traffic has priority on all channels.
4. **Keep transmissions short;** normal conversations are limited to 5 minutes.
5. **A mobile CB with a roof or mirror antenna** in the truck reaches several miles; handhelds much less.

## Warning signs: get help
- Calling for help: "Breaker 9, emergency," then location (mile marker, road, coordinates from the app) and what's wrong.

## Do NOT
- Do not use channel 9 for chatter.
- Do not use illegal amplifiers.

## What you need
- A mobile CB, an antenna mounted on the truck, a power cord.
