## When this applies
Every day of hurricane season and every outage. NOAA Weather Radio keeps broadcasting warnings when cell service and the internet are down.

## Do this
1. **Get a radio with SAME and a tone alert** (and battery, hand-crank or solar power).
2. **Program SAME code 012069 (Lake County)** following the radio's manual. Add 012127 (Volusia) or 012083 (Marion) if you'll be in the forest.
3. **Tune to KIH63 Orlando, 162.475 MHz** (primary for Lake County). **Backup: KIH26 Daytona Beach, 162.400 MHz.**
4. **Leave it on alert mode** at night: it stays silent until a warning for your county, then sounds the 1050 Hz alarm and plays the message.
5. **Test it** on the weekly test (usually Wednesday late morning) and replace batteries each season.
6. Tools → Radio lists these and other frequencies offline.

## Warning signs: get help
- If you program a stronger station that isn't your county's SAME transmitter, you won't get alerts (NWS). Use the stations above.

## Do NOT
- Do not rely on only one way to get warnings: AlertLake, the FEMA app and radio together.

## What you need
- A SAME weather radio (e.g. Midland WR120) plus a crank or solar emergency radio for the go bag.
