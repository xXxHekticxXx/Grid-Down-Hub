## When this applies
The Ocala NF has one of Florida's largest bear populations, and bears regularly come into Sorrento, Umatilla and Mount Plymouth neighborhoods for trash and dog food.

## Do this
1. **Food, trash, dog food and grills:** keep them locked in the truck cab or a hard-sided container, not in the tent or outside the camper. Hang or store food away from where you sleep.
2. **Far away bear:** don't approach; if your presence changes its behavior, you're too close (FWC).
3. **Close range:** stand upright, speak in a calm, firm voice, back slowly toward a secure place, give the bear a clear escape route, avoid direct eye contact. Stop and hold your ground if backing away seems to agitate it.
4. **Huffing, jaw-popping, stomping, bluff charges** are the bear saying "back off," not an attack (FWC).
5. **If a black bear attacks: fight back aggressively** with anything: rocks, sticks, fists, a hatchet.
6. **Keep the dog leashed;** a dog chasing a bear often runs back to you with the bear behind it.
7. **Report** bears threatening people, pets or livestock to FWC (888-404-3922).

## Warning signs: get help
- Any bear injury: first aid and medical care.

## Do NOT
- Do not run (bears sprint up to 35 mph), climb a tree or play dead (FWC).
- Do not feed bears or leave food out; it gets bears killed.

## What you need
- Locking food storage, bear spray if you want an extra layer.
