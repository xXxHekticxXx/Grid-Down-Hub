## When this applies
After a roof leak, flooding or a damp camper in the heat. Mold starts in a day or two.

## Do this
1. **People with asthma, allergies, lung disease or weak immune systems should not do the cleanup** (CDC).
2. **Protect yourself:** an N95 respirator at minimum, gloves, goggles without vents.
3. **Dry it out:** open up, fans or the generator to run a dehumidifier, take wet things out into the sun.
4. **Throw out** porous items that stayed wet more than 48 hours: mattresses, carpet, cushions, drywall, insulation, paper.
5. **Clean hard surfaces** with soap and water, or a bleach solution of **no more than 1 cup of bleach per gallon of water,** with windows open.
6. **Dry thoroughly** and fix the leak.

## Warning signs: get help
- Breathing trouble, wheezing or worsening asthma: get out of the space.

## Do NOT
- **Do not mix bleach with ammonia or any other cleaner.**
- Do not paint over mold.
- Do not sleep in a moldy camper if you have asthma.

## What you need
- N95 masks, gloves, goggles, bleach, buckets, scrub brushes, trash bags, fans.
