## When this applies
After storms, lines are down across roads, fences and trees. They can come back on without warning.

## Do this
1. **Stay at least 35 feet away** and keep the dog leashed. Electricity spreads through wet ground and fences.
2. **Report it** to the utility (SECO Energy or Duke Energy in this area) or 911.
3. **A line on your vehicle: stay inside** and call 911. If you must leave because of fire, jump clear with both feet together without touching the truck and the ground at the same time, then shuffle away with feet together.
4. **Feel tingling in your feet near a line:** shuffle away with small steps, feet together.
5. **Chainsaw work:** don't cut trees touching lines until the utility confirms they're dead (CDC).

## Warning signs: get help
- Someone shocked: don't touch them until the power is off; see [Lightning strike and electrical shock](#/a/lightning-electrical).

## Do NOT
- Do not drive over downed lines or through water that touches them.
- Do not try to move a line with a stick.

## What you need
- The utility's outage number in My Plan → Contacts.
