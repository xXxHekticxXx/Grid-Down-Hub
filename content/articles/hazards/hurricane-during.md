## When this applies
The storm is arriving or over you.

## Do this
1. **Stay inside** your sturdy building, in a small interior room, closet or hallway on the lowest floor that won't flood, away from windows.
2. **Listen to NOAA Weather Radio** (Tools → Radio has the frequencies) or a local AM/FM station on battery.
3. **Tornado warning:** go to the interior room, get low, cover your head and neck with your arms, a mattress or cushions.
4. **Flooding inside:** go to the highest level, but **not into a closed attic** where you can be trapped; take something to break out through if you must go up.
5. **Keep phones charged** and use them only for important calls and texts; texts get through when calls don't.
6. **The eye:** a sudden calm can last minutes to an hour. The worst winds return from the other direction. Stay put until officials say it's over.

## Warning signs: get help
- Water rising fast, roof failing, or injuries: call 911. If 911 can't come, follow [Floods](#/a/floods) and first aid pages.

## Do NOT
- Do not go outside to check damage during the eye.
- Do not run a generator inside or in an attached garage.
- Do not walk, swim or drive through floodwater. Turn around, don't drown.

## What you need
- NOAA radio, flashlights, the go bag, shoes on your feet, the dog leashed.
