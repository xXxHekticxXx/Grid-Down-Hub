## When this applies
A hurricane or other emergency when the camper isn't safe and you have no sturdy building to go to.

## Do this
1. **Confirm which shelters are open via AlertLake** or the **Lake County Citizens Information Line: 352-253-9999.** Not all shelters open for every storm.
2. **Pet-friendly shelters:** Lake County designates some schools as pet-friendly shelters (in Hurricane Milton, 2024: Mascotte, Round Lake, Spring Creek and Treadway Elementary, plus several pet-friendly special-needs sites). Round Lake Elementary is the closest to Sorrento. Bring the dog in a crate with food, bowls, leash, vaccination records and waste bags; pets are usually kept in a separate area.
3. **Map:** open the Map and turn on the Shelters layer; each shelter shows whether it is pet-friendly and a reminder to confirm through AlertLake.
4. **Bring:** the go bag, 3 days of food and water, medicines, bedding (a sleeping pad and blanket), chargers, ID and documents, things to do.
5. **Arrive early:** shelters fill, and roads close when winds pick up.

## Warning signs: get help
- Medical needs that require power or nursing (oxygen, dialysis): register for a special-needs shelter in advance with Lake County EM.

## Do NOT
- Do not bring alcohol, weapons (shelters prohibit them) or illegal drugs.
- Do not show up with the dog at a general-population shelter; go to a pet-friendly one.

## What you need
- Dog crate, go bag, the Shelters map layer, AlertLake sign-up.
