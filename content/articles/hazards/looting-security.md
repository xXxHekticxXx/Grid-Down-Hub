## When this applies
Long outages and shortages bring theft (generators, fuel, batteries) and, in some places, desperation. Rural property on 10 acres is visible from the road and has things people want.

## Do this
1. **Low profile:** don't advertise supplies. Run the generator only as needed, keep lights shielded at night, keep fuel and gear out of sight from the road.
2. **Neighbors are your best security.** Check on them, share information, agree on signals, take turns watching. A known community is a hard target.
3. **Lock and chain** the generator, fuel cans, the truck and tools. Motion-sensor solar lights near the camper and gate.
4. **The dog** is an excellent alarm; keep him close at night.
5. **Gate:** keep it closed and locked; know who's coming and going.
6. **Avoid crowds** at gas lines and distribution points late in the day; go early, go with someone, don't flash cash.
7. **Comms:** FRS or GMRS radios with neighbors; see [FRS and GMRS radios](#/a/frs-gmrs).
8. **Firearms:** secure them in the camper (heat, humidity, theft); carry within Florida law; see [Firearms in the field](#/a/firearms-in-the-field). Use of deadly force is only lawful under strict conditions in Florida law (chapter 776); avoid confrontation whenever you can.

## Warning signs: get help
- Credible threats: call 911 or the Lake County Sheriff's non-emergency line; document and leave if you need to.

## Do NOT
- Do not confront thieves over property.
- Do not set traps or booby traps on your property (illegal and dangerous).
- Do not display or brandish firearms (Florida 790.10).

## What you need
- Chains and padlocks, solar motion lights, FRS/GMRS radios, a neighbor contact list in My Plan.
