## When this applies
Heavy tropical rain, a hurricane or a stalled storm floods low roads, ditches, lakeshores and the low parts of the property.

## Do this
1. **Know which parts of the 10 acres and which roads flood** before you need to: watch them in a heavy summer storm.
2. **Flood warning:** move to higher ground, or stay where you are if you're already high and safe (Ready.gov).
3. **Park the truck on the highest ground** you have, facing out.
4. **Driving:** if water covers the road, turn around. You can't see if the road is washed out under it.
5. **Trapped in a vehicle in rising water:** get out and onto the roof if water is rising inside; call 911.
6. **After:** stay out of floodwater; wash with soap and clean water if you touched it; cover wounds with waterproof bandages.

## Warning signs: get help
- Water rising around the camper: leave early, while you still can drive out.
- A wound exposed to floodwater that turns red, swollen or oozing: see a doctor (Vibrio and other infections).

## Do NOT
- Do not walk, swim or drive through floodwater (Ready.gov).
- Do not let the dog swim in floodwater.
- Do not drive across bridges over fast-moving water.

## What you need
- Waterproof boots, a walking stick to probe ahead if you must cross shallow water, waterproof bandages, see [Crossing water on foot](#/a/water-crossings).
