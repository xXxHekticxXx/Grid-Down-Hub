## When this applies
A week or two after heavy rain and flooding, mosquito numbers explode. Florida has had local cases of West Nile, eastern equine encephalitis and dengue. Dogs get heartworm from mosquitoes.

## Do this
1. **Empty standing water weekly:** buckets, tarps, tires, the dog's outdoor bowl, rain catchment without lids.
2. **Repellent (CDC):** EPA-registered products with DEET, picaridin, IR3535, oil of lemon eucalyptus, PMD or 2-undecanone. Follow the label; sunscreen first, repellent second.
3. **Clothing:** long sleeves and pants at dawn and dusk; permethrin-treated clothes and gear.
4. **Screens:** keep the camper's screens patched; a mosquito net over the bed if they're torn or you're camping.
5. **The dog:** keep him on heartworm prevention.

## Warning signs: get help
- Fever, headache, body aches, rash, stiff neck or confusion within two weeks of many bites: see a doctor.

## Do NOT
- Do not rely on citronella candles, wristbands or unregistered "natural" repellents alone (CDC).
- Do not use OLE or PMD on children under 3.

## What you need
- DEET or picaridin repellent, permethrin, a head net, a bed net, screen patch kit. See also [Mosquitoes and biting insects](#/a/insect-protection).
