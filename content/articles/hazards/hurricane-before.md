## When this applies
Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30. Lake County is inland, but hurricanes still bring damaging wind, tornadoes, flooding and long power outages here (Ready.gov: hurricanes are not just a coastal problem).

## Do this
**Every year before June:**
1. **Sign up for AlertLake** (Lake County's free alerts by text, call and email) and the FEMA app.
2. **Decide where you'll ride out a strong storm.** A camper is not safe in hurricane-force winds or tornadoes. Pick a sturdy building now: a friend's or family's concrete-block house, or a [Lake County shelter](#/a/shelters-lake-county) (several are pet-friendly; confirm which are open through AlertLake).
3. **Stock up:** 2 weeks of water and food, medicines, the dog's supplies, batteries, fuel. Use the Hurricane checklist.
4. **Documents:** photos of ID, insurance and vehicle title, and the dog's records, saved in My Plan; originals in a waterproof bag.
5. **Property:** trim dead limbs near the camper, clear gutters and drains, know how to shut off power, propane and water.

**When a storm is forecast (3 to 5 days out):**
1. **Fill up:** truck tank, gas cans, propane, the camper's fresh tank, every water jug. Get cash.
2. **Charge everything:** phone, power banks, the power station, radios.
3. **Secure or put away** everything that can fly: chairs, grills, tools, trash cans, the awning. Tie down or stake what stays.
4. **Freeze water jugs;** set fridge and freezer to coldest.
5. **Download** anything else you want offline and check this app's map packs (Setup).
6. **Move the truck** away from trees and low spots.
7. **Go to your safe building before the winds arrive** (tropical-storm-force winds arrive well before the eye). Take the go bag, the dog and his kit.

## Warning signs: get help
- An evacuation order for your area: go. Officials in Lake County may order evacuation of mobile homes and RVs for strong storms.
- Tornado warnings come with hurricanes; see [Tornadoes](#/a/tornadoes).

## Do NOT
- Do not stay in the camper for a hurricane or a strong tropical storm.
- Do not wait until the last day to buy fuel and water; stations run dry.
- Do not tape windows; it doesn't help.
