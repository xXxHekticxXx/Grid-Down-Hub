## When this applies
Bugging out or camping in the Ocala National Forest, or walking out if the truck can't go on.

## Do this
1. **Truck:** at least 3 gallons per person and 2 gallons for the dog per 3 days. Stack jugs low and secured.
2. **On you:** the canteen full, plus a collapsible bottle or bladder. A full canteen weighs about 2 lb a quart; water is the heaviest thing you'll carry.
3. **Refill at every safe chance,** before you need it. Springs in the forest are your main source; the map shows them.
4. **Treat everything:** LifeStraw for drinking from a bottle; a squeeze filter to fill containers; bleach or chlorine dioxide for water near homes or roads.
5. **Drink on a schedule** in heat (a few swallows every 15 to 20 minutes) and check your urine: pale yellow is good.
6. **Camp near water but 100+ feet back from it** (Forest Service rule and alligator sense).

## Warning signs: get help
- Dizziness, headache, dark urine or no urine: you're behind. Rest in shade and drink. See [Heat exhaustion](#/a/heat-exhaustion).

## Do NOT
- Do not drink untreated water because it "looks clear."
- Do not leave water in clear bottles in the sun for weeks (algae grows).

## What you need
- Canteen, LifeStraw, a squeeze filter with spare bags, bleach in a small dropper bottle, 5-gallon jugs in the truck.
