## When this applies
Your utility or the county issues a boil water notice (pressure loss, main break, flooding, power loss at the plant). Notices come by AlertLake, local radio and the utility.

## Do this
1. **Drinking, cooking, making ice, baby formula, brushing teeth, washing produce:** use only bottled, boiled (1 minute rolling boil) or properly disinfected water.
2. **Dishes:** use boiled or disinfected water, or a dishwasher with a sanitize cycle, or a final rinse in a bleach solution (1 tablespoon per gallon).
3. **Showering:** fine for adults; don't swallow it. Sponge-bathe babies.
4. **Throw out ice** made since the notice began.
5. **When the notice is lifted:** run cold taps for several minutes, flush the camper's water lines, and replace filters (fridge, under-sink).

## Warning signs: get help
- Diarrhea or vomiting in the household during a notice: see [Diarrhea and oral rehydration](#/a/diarrhea-ors).

## Do NOT
- Do not rely on a pitcher or fridge filter: most don't remove germs.

## What you need
- Bottled water, a pot and fuel, bleach and a dropper.
