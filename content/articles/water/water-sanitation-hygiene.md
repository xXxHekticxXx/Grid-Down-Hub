## When this applies
Running water is off or limited, and you're cooking, cleaning wounds, using a bucket toilet or caring for someone sick.

## Do this
1. **Tippy-tap or jug station:** a jug with a spigot on a table, soap on a string, a bucket below for gray water.
2. **Wash hands with soap and water** before cooking or eating, after the toilet, after handling garbage or animals, and before and after wound care. 20 seconds, then air-dry or a clean towel.
3. **No water:** alcohol hand sanitizer with at least 60% alcohol, after wiping off visible dirt.
4. **Dishes:** scrape, wash in hot soapy water, rinse, then sanitize in a tub with about 1 tablespoon of unscented bleach per gallon of water for a minute, and air dry.
5. **Bathing:** a washcloth and a basin of water: face, armpits, groin and feet every day prevents rashes and infections in the heat.
6. **Brushing teeth:** use treated water only.
7. **Gray water:** pour it away from the water supply and camp, at least 100 feet from springs and lakes.

## Warning signs: get help
- Several people with diarrhea at once: look for the common source (water, a food) and stop using it.

## Do NOT
- Do not share towels or drinking cups when someone is sick.
- Do not wash dishes or yourself in springs or lakes with soap.

## What you need
- A 5-gallon jug with a spigot, bar soap, sanitizer, dish tubs, unscented bleach, washcloths.
