## When this applies
Tap water after a boil-water notice, well water after flooding, rainwater, or water from a lake, spring, river or ditch.

## Do this
**Cloudy water first:** let it settle, then pour the clear water through a clean cloth, paper towel or coffee filter.

**Boil (best, kills everything):**
1. Bring to a **rolling boil for 1 minute** (3 minutes above 6,500 ft, not an issue in Florida).
2. Cool, store in clean covered containers. Pour back and forth between containers or add a pinch of salt per quart if it tastes flat.

**Bleach (when you can't boil):**
1. Use plain unscented bleach, 5 to 9% sodium hypochlorite (check the label).
2. **8 drops per gallon (about ½ mL); 40 drops (½ teaspoon) per 5 gallons.** Double it for cloudy, colored or very cold water: 16 drops per gallon, 80 per 5 gallons.
3. Stir, **wait at least 30 minutes.** It should smell slightly of chlorine; if not, repeat the dose and wait 15 more minutes.
4. EPA's table allows 6 drops per gallon for 8.25% bleach; CDC's 8 drops covers all 5 to 9% bleach. Bleach older than about a year loses strength.

**Filters (LifeStraw, Sawyer and similar):**
- A filter with an absolute pore size of 1 micron or smaller (or NSF 53/58 cyst rated) removes parasites; 0.3 micron or smaller removes bacteria too. **Most portable filters do not remove viruses.**
- **Best field method: filter, then disinfect** the filtered water with bleach or chlorine dioxide tablets. Chlorine dioxide also kills Giardia and is somewhat effective on Cryptosporidium.
- Your LifeStraw is excellent for parasites and bacteria from springs and lakes; for anything that might have sewage in it (floodwater, ditches near homes), filter and then add bleach.

**Iodine:** 5 drops of 2% tincture of iodine per quart (10 if cloudy), wait 30 minutes (EPA). Not for pregnant women or long-term use.

## Warning signs: get help
- Water that smells of fuel or chemicals, has a sheen, or comes from near a flooded industrial or farm site: boiling and bleach don't remove chemicals. Find another source.
- Diarrhea or vomiting after drinking: see [Diarrhea and oral rehydration](#/a/diarrhea-ors).

## Do NOT
- Do not use scented, color-safe or "splash-less" bleach.
- Do not trust clear spring water untreated; germs are invisible.
- Do not let the LifeStraw freeze or the filtered end touch dirty water.

## What you need
- Unscented bleach and a dropper, a pot and stove fuel, your LifeStraw plus a squeeze filter for volume, chlorine dioxide tablets, coffee filters, clean containers.
