## When this applies
Summer afternoons bring storms almost daily in Central Florida. A good collection setup can supply washing and drinking water when wells and taps fail.

## Do this
1. **Catchment:** the camper roof, a shed roof or a tarp tied with one corner lower than the rest, draining into a bucket or barrel.
2. **First flush:** let the first minute or two of rain run off to rinse dust, pollen and droppings, then start collecting.
3. **Screen it:** a cloth over the bucket keeps leaves and mosquitoes out.
4. **Cover stored rainwater** so mosquitoes can't breed (a full barrel without a lid becomes a breeding site within a week).
5. **Treat before drinking:** filter and bleach or boil. Rain itself is fairly clean; roofs are not.
6. **Rain is ideal untreated for washing, flushing and the garden.**

## Warning signs: get help
- Water from asphalt shingles or treated wood roofs can carry chemicals; use it for washing and flushing, not drinking.

## Do NOT
- Do not leave open containers of water standing (mosquitoes).
- Do not collect during lightning on a metal roof or ladder; see [Lightning](#/a/lightning).

## What you need
- A tarp with grommets, cord, a few 5-gallon buckets with lids, a cloth screen, bleach.
