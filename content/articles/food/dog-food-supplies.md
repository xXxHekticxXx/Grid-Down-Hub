## When this applies
Before hurricane season and before bugging out. Many shelters and hotels don't allow pets; Lake County has pet-friendly shelters, but plan for him to be with you.

## Do this
1. **Food:** at least 2 weeks of his regular food. A 90-lb dog eats roughly 4 to 5 cups of kibble a day (check his bag's chart); a 40-lb bag lasts about 4 weeks.
2. **Store it** in an airtight bin, the bag inside the bin (keeps the lot number and date for recalls), in a cool spot.
3. **Water:** about 1 ounce per pound per day, more in heat; see [How much water to store](#/a/water-storage).
4. **Medicines and preventives:** flea, tick and heartworm medicine, plus any prescriptions, 30 days.
5. **Records:** rabies certificate, vaccination records, microchip number and a current photo of him in My Plan → Dog (Ready.gov recommends microchipping and current contact info).
6. **Kit:** bowls, leash and spare collar with tags, a crate that fits in the truck, waste bags, a towel, the dog first aid kit.
7. **Changing food:** switch gradually over several days to avoid diarrhea.

## Warning signs: get help
- Vomiting, diarrhea for more than a day, not eating, or drinking much more than usual: call the vet.

## Do NOT
- Do not feed him cooked bones, onions, grapes or raisins, chocolate, xylitol (sugar-free gum) or table scraps with lots of fat.
- Do not leave him behind in an evacuation (Ready.gov).
