## When this applies
Using up stored cans, especially after heat, flooding or long storage.

## Do this
1. **Check every can before opening:** throw out cans that are bulging, leaking, badly rusted, or deeply dented, especially dents on the top or side seams.
2. **When opened,** throw it out if liquid spurts, it foams, or it smells bad or looks moldy.
3. **High-acid canned foods** (tomatoes, fruit) keep best quality 12 to 18 months; **low-acid** (meat, vegetables, beans) 2 to 5 years if stored cool. Past the date they are often safe if the can is in good shape, but quality drops.
4. **Home-canned low-acid foods** (vegetables, meat) can carry botulism if they weren't pressure-canned: boil them 10 minutes before eating if you have any doubt about how they were processed.
5. **Bag and dispose of suspect cans** without opening them; wash hands and surfaces.

## Warning signs: get help
- Botulism signs, hours to days after eating: double or blurred vision, drooping eyelids, slurred speech, trouble swallowing, muscle weakness moving down the body. **Emergency: 911.**

## Do NOT
- **Do not taste suspicious food.**
- Do not use cans that floodwater touched without sanitizing them ([Food touched by floodwater](#/a/food-after-flood)).

## What you need
- Good storage conditions: cool, dry, off the floor.
