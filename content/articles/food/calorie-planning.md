## When this applies
An outage or evacuation that is lasting longer than your plan, with resupply uncertain.

## Do this
1. **Inventory everything** in Tools → Inventory, then divide by how many people and days you need to cover.
2. **Eat the perishables first,** then frozen, then shelf-stable.
3. **Stretch with bulk staples:** rice, beans, oats, pasta and flour feed far more than canned meals for the weight and cost.
4. **Keep protein and fat in the diet** (canned meat, peanut butter, oil, nuts, fish); carbs alone leave you hungry and weak.
5. **Reduce what you burn:** heavy work early and late, rest in the heat of the day.
6. **Add what's around:** fish, legal game and [wild edible plants](#/a/wild-edibles-rules) you are certain of.
7. **Vitamins:** a daily multivitamin covers gaps in a canned-food diet.

## Warning signs: get help
- Dizziness, confusion, fainting or rapid weight loss: eat more and rest. Children and older adults are affected faster.

## Do NOT
- Do not cut water to save food; water comes first (you can go weeks without food, days without water).
- Do not eat plants or mushrooms you can't identify with certainty.

## What you need
- The Inventory tool, a multivitamin, staples bought in bulk.
