## When this applies
Setting up or rotating food supplies before hurricane season or a long outage.

## Do this
1. **Choose food you eat now** so it gets rotated: canned meat, tuna and chicken; canned beans, chili, soups, vegetables and fruit; peanut butter; crackers; oatmeal; rice and pasta; powdered milk; nuts and dried fruit; honey; coffee and drink mixes; salt and seasonings.
2. **Plan about 2,000 to 2,500 calories a day per adult,** more for hard physical work. Canned meat and peanut butter carry the most calories per dollar and weight.
3. **Mix no-cook foods** (ready to eat from the can) with foods that need only boiling water (oatmeal, ramen, instant rice) for when fuel is short.
4. **Store cool and dry:** inside the coolest part of the camper or a shed out of sun, off the floor. Heat shortens shelf life a lot; Florida summer sheds can top 120 °F.
5. **Seal against bugs and moisture:** rice, flour and pasta in buckets with gasket lids or Mylar bags with oxygen absorbers. Boxed food in sealed bins (Ready.gov).
6. **Rotate:** first in, first out. Write dates on everything; check twice a year (hurricane season start and end).
7. **Manual can opener,** two of them.

## Warning signs: get help
- Bulging, leaking, or spurting cans, or a sour smell when opened: throw them out without tasting ([Canned food safety](#/a/canned-food-safety)).

## Do NOT
- Do not stock a lot of salty food without extra water to go with it.
- Do not store food next to fuel, pesticides or the generator.

## What you need
- Food-grade buckets with gasket lids, Mylar bags and oxygen absorbers, a marker, two can openers.
- The Inventory tool (Tools → Inventory) tracks quantities and expiration dates.
