## When this applies
No electric stove or microwave: during outages, at camp, or bugging out.

## Do this
1. **Outside only:** propane camp stoves, charcoal grills, rocket stoves and fires go outdoors, away from the camper and awning, never in a closed space (carbon monoxide). See [Carbon monoxide poisoning](#/a/carbon-monoxide-poisoning).
2. **One-pot meals save fuel:** rice and canned chicken; pasta with canned sauce; beans and rice; oatmeal. Use lids, turn the flame down once boiling.
3. **Soak beans and rice** for hours beforehand to cut cooking time.
4. **Retained-heat cooking:** bring to a boil, then wrap the covered pot in a sleeping bag or put it in a cooler packed with towels for an hour or two; it finishes cooking without fuel.
5. **Cook meat to safe temperatures** with a thermometer: see [Safe cooking temperatures](#/a/safe-cooking-temps).
6. **Keep a fire extinguisher** near the cooking area.

## Warning signs: get help
- Headache, dizziness or nausea while cooking: get to fresh air; it may be CO.
- Burns: [Burns](#/a/burns).

## Do NOT
- Do not cook or heat with a camp stove or grill inside the camper, a tent, the truck or a garage.
- Do not leave a fire or stove unattended.
- Do not use gasoline to start a grill or fire.

## What you need
- A two-burner propane stove and 1-lb cylinders plus an adapter hose for a 20-lb tank, a charcoal grill and charcoal, matches and lighters, a pot with lid, a cast iron skillet, a meat thermometer, a fire extinguisher.
