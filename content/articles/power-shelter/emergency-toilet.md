## When this applies
The camper's black tank is full and you can't dump, the water is off, or you're camping.

## Do this
1. **Bucket toilet:** a 5-gallon bucket with a snap-on toilet seat, lined with two heavy trash bags.
2. **After each use:** a scoop of kitty litter, sawdust, peat moss or lime to absorb liquid and cut odor.
3. **Tie off the inner bag** when it's full, put in a new one, store used bags in a lidded trash can away from the camper, water and the dog, until trash service or a safe disposal option returns.
4. **Urine separately** if you can (a jug or a separate bucket); it greatly reduces odor and bag use. Pour urine on the ground away from water sources.
5. **Wash hands** every time ([Hygiene with little water](#/a/water-sanitation-hygiene)).
6. **Field:** dig a cat hole **6 inches deep, at least 100 feet from water,** trails and camp; fill and cover it (Forest Service rule). Pack out toilet paper or bury it deep.
7. **Camper tanks:** conserve: use the bucket for solids, the camper toilet for urine, and dump only at proper stations.

## Warning signs: get help
- Diarrhea spreading in the group: tighten hygiene, separate the sick person's toilet if possible.

## Do NOT
- Do not dump black tanks or waste on the ground or into ditches, springs or lakes.
- Do not put waste bags where floodwater can reach them.

## What you need
- Two 5-gallon buckets, a toilet seat lid, heavy contractor bags, kitty litter, a trowel for the field, sanitizer.
