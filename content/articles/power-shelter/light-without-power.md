## When this applies
Every night of an outage.

## Do this
1. **Headlamp for each person** (hands-free) plus one LED lantern per room.
2. **Batteries:** standardize on AA and 18650 or USB-rechargeable lights so the power station or a solar charger keeps them going. Keep alkaline spares for long storage.
3. **Solar lanterns and garden lights** charge by day and light the camper at night.
4. **Red light mode** saves night vision and battery; the app's Night mode does the same on your phone.
5. **This phone as a flashlight:** Tools → Flashlight.

## Warning signs: get help
- Burns or fire from candles: [Burns](#/a/burns).

## Do NOT
- Do not use candles in the camper unattended or near curtains; a candle fire is common after storms.
- Do not use propane or kerosene lanterns inside an enclosed camper or tent (CO and fire).

## What you need
- Headlamps, LED lanterns, rechargeable batteries and charger, solar lanterns, spare alkalines.
