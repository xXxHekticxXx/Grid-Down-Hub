## When this applies
Keeping fuel for the truck, generator, chainsaw and stoves, which matters most because gas stations stop pumping without power.

## Do this
1. **Containers:** approved red gasoline cans (yellow diesel, blue kerosene) with proper spouts; never food containers.
2. **Stabilizer** in gasoline when you buy it; stabilized gas keeps about 1 year, untreated about 3 to 6 months. Ethanol-free gas keeps best for small engines.
3. **Rotate:** pour stored gas into the truck every 6 to 12 months and refill.
4. **Store** in a ventilated shed or a shaded outdoor spot, off the ground, away from the camper, the generator and any flame or pilot light. Florida heat raises vapor pressure; vent cans carefully.
5. **Propane:** tanks upright, outdoors, valves closed, chained so they can't tip, away from the camper door. Never store propane indoors or in the truck cab.
6. **Fill before storms:** truck full, every can full, every propane tank swapped.
7. **Track** what you have: Inventory, and Tools → Fuel range to see how far it gets you.

## Warning signs: get help
- Fuel smell inside the camper or truck cab: find and fix the leak; ventilate.
- Fuel on skin or in eyes: [Chemicals on skin or in the eye](#/a/chemical-exposure). Swallowed: [Poisoning](#/a/poisoning).

## Do NOT
- Do not siphon by mouth; use a pump.
- Do not fill cans in the truck bed (static): set them on the ground.
- Do not smoke or use a phone while handling fuel near vapors.

## What you need
- Approved cans, fuel stabilizer, a siphon pump, a funnel, chains for propane tanks.
