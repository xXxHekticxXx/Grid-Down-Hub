## When this applies
The phone is your map, library, radio guide and flashlight. Without power, keeping it charged is a daily job.

## Do this
1. **Before the outage:** install every map pack in Setup and open each section once; charge the phone, power banks and power station to 100%.
2. **Turn on this app's Battery saver** (Settings): simpler screens, dark theme, less animation.
3. **Phone settings:** battery saver mode, lowest comfortable brightness, dark mode, background refresh off.
4. **Airplane mode** when there's no signal: searching for towers drains the battery fast. **GPS still works in airplane mode** if location is on.
5. **Check for signal** at set times: turn airplane mode off for a few minutes, send texts (they get through when calls don't), then back on.
6. **Charge from:** the truck's USB or a 12 V adapter while driving, the power station, a solar panel with USB output, power banks.
7. **Protect it:** a waterproof case or zip bag, out of the sun.

## Warning signs: get help
- A swollen or hot phone battery: stop charging it.

## Do NOT
- Do not leave the phone on the truck dashboard in the sun.
- Do not wait until 5% to charge; keep it topped up whenever power is available.

## What you need
- Two power banks (20,000 mAh), cables (USB-C), a 12 V USB charger for the truck, a small solar panel with USB.
