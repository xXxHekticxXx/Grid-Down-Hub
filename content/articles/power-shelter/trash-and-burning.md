## When this applies
Trash pickup stops for days or weeks after a storm.

## Do this
1. **Reduce:** crush cans, flatten boxes, keep food scraps separate.
2. **Food trash:** double-bagged, in a locking or strapped can, ideally in a shed or the truck bed under a lid; bears and raccoons will tear into anything that smells of food (FWC).
3. **Burying food scraps** at least 2 feet deep away from water works on acreage.
4. **Burning yard debris:** only clean, dry vegetation, in a cleared area, small piles, with water and a shovel ready, and only when burning is allowed (no burn bans, low wind). Florida Forest Service rules apply to yard debris burning.
5. **Flies and rats:** keep trash sealed and away from the camper; clean up spilled dog food.

## Warning signs: get help
- A fire spreading beyond the pile: call 911.

## Do NOT
- Do not burn plastics, foam, treated or painted wood, tires, batteries, or poison ivy and oleander (toxic smoke).
- Do not burn on dry, windy days.

## What you need
- Heavy trash bags, a locking can, a burn barrel with a screen lid, a shovel and water.
