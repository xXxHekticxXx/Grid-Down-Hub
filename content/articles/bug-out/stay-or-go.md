## When this applies
A long grid-down event, a storm or a threat, and you're deciding whether to stay at the 10 acres or leave for the Ocala NF or another destination. Open the Stay and Go plans from Home.

## Do this
**Stay if most of these are true:**
- The camper is intact and the property isn't flooding or burning.
- You have water (stored or well with power) and at least 2 weeks of food.
- No evacuation order for your area.
- Neighbors are steady and you can secure the property.
- Roads out are jammed, fuel is scarce, or the destination is no better.

**Go if any of these is true:**
- **An evacuation order** covers you (mobile homes and RVs are often ordered out first for hurricanes).
- Fire, flood or storm damage makes the camper unsafe.
- Water has run out with no way to make more.
- Credible threats to your safety where you are.
- Someone needs medical care you can only reach by moving.

**Decide early.** The best time to leave is before everyone else does, with a full tank and daylight.

**If you go:**
1. Follow the **Go plan** (Home → Go): load list, route, fuel, meeting points.
2. Leave a note at the camper (where, when, route).
3. Tell your out-of-area contact.
4. Lock up and shut off propane.

## Warning signs: get help
- Conditions changing fast (water rising, fire approaching): stop deciding and go.

## Do NOT
- Do not leave without a destination, enough fuel to get there, and water.
- Do not drive into a storm, smoke or floodwater to "beat it."

## What you need
- The Stay plan (Home → Stay) and Go plan (Home → Go), the Truck load and Stay home checklists.
