## When this applies
Driving out during an evacuation or long outage, when stations are closed or rationed.

## Do this
1. **Know your numbers:** tank size, real loaded mpg (lower than normal with a full bed), and gallons in cans. Enter them in the fuel tool above; it gives your range in miles.
2. **Fill up at half a tank,** every chance you get.
3. **Carry cans** to roughly double your range, stabilized and strapped down outside the cab.
4. **Drive smooth and at 55 to 60 mph;** fuel use climbs steeply at highway speed with a load.
5. **Map → Gas layer** shows stations; many won't pump without power. Larger stations on main highways are more likely to have generators.
6. **Cash:** card readers fail without power.
7. **Siphon pump and funnel** to refill from cans.

## Warning signs: get help
- Under a quarter tank and no open stations ahead: stop somewhere safe and reassess rather than running dry on the road.

## Do NOT
- Do not idle for hours in traffic; shut off in long jams.
- Do not fill cans inside the truck bed (static).

## What you need
- Approved cans, stabilizer, siphon pump, funnel, cash.
