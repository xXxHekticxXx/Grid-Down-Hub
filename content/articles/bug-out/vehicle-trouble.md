## When this applies
Your truck breaks down or gets stuck far from help, often on forest sand roads.

## Do this
1. **Get off the road** safely; hazards on; if on a highway, everyone out and away from traffic, behind the guardrail.
2. **Flat tire:** know where the jack, lug wrench and spare are now; practice once. Loosen lugs before jacking; jack on firm ground (a board under the jack on sand).
3. **Dead battery:** a lithium jump pack starts the truck without a second vehicle. Keep it charged.
4. **Overheating:** pull over, engine off, wait for it to cool before opening the radiator cap; top up coolant or water; check for a broken belt or hose.
5. **Stuck in sand:** stop spinning; dig sand from in front of the tires; air down to about 15 to 20 psi for traction (re-inflate after with a 12 V compressor); put floor mats, branches or traction boards under the drive wheels; ease out in low gear.
6. **Tire repair:** a plug kit and 12 V compressor fix most punctures.
7. **Can't fix it:** stay with the truck if help is likely; it's shelter and easy to spot. Signal ([Signaling for help](#/a/signaling-for-help)).

## Warning signs: get help
- Smoke, fuel smell, or brake failure: stop driving.

## Do NOT
- Do not get under a vehicle held only by a jack.
- Do not open a hot radiator cap.

## What you need
- Jack, lug wrench, inflated spare, plug kit, 12 V compressor, jump pack, tow strap, shovel, traction boards, coolant, oil, duct tape, zip ties, basic tools.
