## When this applies
Carrying, transporting and storing the AR-15, the Glock 9mm and the .22 during an emergency or a bug-out.

## Do this
**Safety rules, every time:**
1. Treat every gun as loaded.
2. Never point it at anything you aren't willing to destroy.
3. Finger off the trigger until on target and ready to fire.
4. Know your target and what's behind it.

**Florida law (check current law; it changes):**
1. **Concealed carry:** since July 1, 2023, an adult who meets the eligibility criteria for a concealed weapons license may carry concealed without one; carry valid ID and show it to law enforcement on request (s. 790.01, 790.013).
2. **Open carry:** in September 2025 Florida's First District Court of Appeal struck down the open-carry ban (McDaniels v. State), and the Attorney General told law enforcement not to enforce it. Prohibited places (s. 790.06(12)) and private property owners' rules still apply.
3. **Vehicle:** a handgun securely encased or not readily accessible, or on your person if you're eligible to carry; long guns may be carried in a private vehicle for lawful use (s. 790.25(4)).
4. **Evacuating under a mandatory evacuation order:** lawful concealed carry while evacuating, within 48 hours of the order (s. 790.01(5)(a)).
5. **No "exhibiting" in a rude, careless, angry or threatening manner** (s. 790.10); self-defense law is chapter 776.
6. **Prohibited places** include schools and courthouses; **emergency shelters** generally don't allow weapons.
7. **Hunting** needs a license and season; WMA rules apply in the Ocala NF.

**Storage and care:**
1. Locked when not on you: a lock box bolted in the camper or truck, cable locks.
2. Humidity rusts guns fast: oil them, use desiccant in cases, check weekly.
3. Ammo cool and dry in sealed cans.
4. Clean after shooting and after rain.

## Warning signs: get help
- Gunshot injury: [Gunshot wounds](#/a/gunshot-wound).

## Do NOT
- Do not shoot near roads, campsites, houses or across water; on Forest Service roads in the WMA, don't transport loaded firearms.
- Do not leave firearms in an unlocked truck or tent.
- Do not drink and handle firearms.

## What you need
- Lock box, cable locks, cleaning kit and oil, desiccant packs, ammo cans, eye and ear protection, the trauma kit.
