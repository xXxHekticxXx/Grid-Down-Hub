## When this applies
Getting ready to leave, or packing the truck ahead of hurricane season so it can be done in 15 minutes.

## Do this
1. **Order of priority:** water, the trauma kit and first aid, medicines, documents and cash, the dog and his kit, the go bag, firearms and ammo secured, shelter and sleep gear, food, tools, fuel.
2. **Pack heavy low and forward** (water jugs, fuel cans, ammo) and strap everything down; loose gear becomes projectiles in a crash.
3. **Fuel cans** in the bed, upright, strapped, never in the cab.
4. **Keep reachable in the cab:** trauma kit, water, the map (phone and printed topo sheets), a flashlight, the radio, snacks, the dog's leash.
5. **Firearms:** legally carried or securely encased per Florida law; see [Firearms in the field](#/a/firearms-in-the-field).
6. **Cover and lock** the bed if you can; keep gear out of sight.
7. **Pre-stage:** keep the truck at half a tank or more during hurricane season, and keep bins packed and labeled so they're grab-and-go.
8. **Run through the Truck load checklist** (Checklists) and tick it off.

## Warning signs: get help
- Overloaded truck (sagging, poor braking): drop non-essentials; check tire pressure for the load.

## Do NOT
- Do not stack gear so it blocks the rear view or the dog's space.
- Do not carry gasoline inside the cab.

## What you need
- Ratchet straps, cargo net, labeled bins, the Truck load checklist.
