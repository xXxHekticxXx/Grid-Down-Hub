## When this applies
Leaving fast (fire, flood, threat) or the truck can't go farther and you walk.

## Do this
1. **One pack per person, under about 25 to 30 lb,** packed and by the door.
2. **Contents (see the Go bag checklist):** water (2 liters) plus the LifeStraw and a squeeze filter; 3 days of light food; trauma kit (tourniquet, gauze, bandage) and a small first aid kit; headlamp; storm matches, lighter, ferro rod; tarp, cord, emergency blanket; poncho; change of socks and a shirt; knife; whistle; small radio; phone power bank and cable; copies of ID and documents in a zip bag; 7 days of medicines; cash in small bills; N95 masks; the dog's leash and a day of his food.
3. **Check it** at the start and end of hurricane season: rotate food and water, test lights, swap batteries.
4. **Keep the truck version bigger** (Truck load checklist).

## Warning signs: get help
- If you can't carry it a mile comfortably, it's too heavy.

## Do NOT
- Do not pack gear you've never used.

## What you need
- A sturdy backpack, the Go bag checklist.
