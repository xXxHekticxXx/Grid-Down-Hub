## When this applies
Evacuating, bugging out or camping with your 90-lb dog.

## Do this
1. **In the truck:** a crate strapped down or a crash-tested harness clipped to a seatbelt; not loose in the bed.
2. **Water:** about ¾ gallon a day for him, more in heat; offer it at every stop (every 2 hours).
3. **Heat:** walk him on grass or sand early and late; hot pavement burns paws; never leave him in a parked truck in warm weather.
4. **ID:** collar with tags and your cell number, microchip registered with current info, a recent photo of him in My Plan.
5. **Records:** rabies certificate and vaccinations (shelters and campgrounds ask).
6. **Shelters:** only pet-friendly shelters accept him; see [Lake County shelters](#/a/shelters-lake-county).
7. **Camp:** leashed or on a tie-out away from the water's edge (alligators), off ant mounds, under the bug net if possible; feed and store food where bears can't get it.
8. **Stress:** keep his routine, his bed or blanket, and talk to him; frightened dogs bolt.

## Warning signs: get help
- Heatstroke, snakebite, injury: [Dog first aid](#/a/dog-first-aid).

## Do NOT
- Do not let him drink from lakes, ditches or floodwater; treat his water like yours.
- Do not leave him behind.

## What you need
- Crate or harness, leash and spare, tie-out cable, bowls, 2 weeks of food, his kit and records. The Dog supplies checklist.
