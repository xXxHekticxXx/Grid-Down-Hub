## When this applies
Bugging out to, or camping in, the Ocala NF (north of Umatilla, about 30 to 60 minutes from Sorrento).

## Do this
1. **Dispersed camping rules (Forest Service):** camping is generally limited to **14 days in a 30-day period;** pick a previously used site; not near developed recreation areas or **within 100 feet of streams and water;** pets leashed; groups over 75 need a permit.
2. **Gun season:** during general gun season (FWC Ocala WMA dates, usually fall to winter), **camping is allowed only in designated campsites.**
3. **Fire:** check Forest Service alerts and orders. In March 2026 the forest was under **Stage 1 fire restrictions:** dispersed campers could use propane only (charcoal allowed for grilling); campfires only in developed sites with provided rings. Restrictions change; assume restrictions in dry season.
4. **Campgrounds:** Alexander Springs, Juniper Springs, Salt Springs, Clearwater Lake and others have water, toilets and fees; they fill fast and may close after storms.
5. **Hunt camps** are open only in hunting season with a permit; some (Farles Lake, Clay Lake, South Tower) are closed.
6. **Waste:** pack out trash, bury human waste 6 inches deep 100 feet from water.
7. **Bears and gators:** lock food in the truck; keep the dog leashed away from water; see [Black bears](#/a/black-bears) and [Alligators](#/a/alligators).
8. **Firearms:** the forest is a wildlife management area; transporting firearms to the shooting range is limited to state and county roads; FWC WMA rules apply. See [Firearms in the field](#/a/firearms-in-the-field).
9. **No residency:** establishing residence on national forest land is illegal; a long-term plan needs private land or another arrangement.

## Warning signs: get help
- Hurricane damage closures: the Forest Service closed several Ocala sites after recent storms; check before relying on a campground.

## Do NOT
- Do not cut standing trees or limbs; use dead and down wood.
- Do not camp in clearings or meadows (Forest Service asks you not to).

## What you need
- The map with Campgrounds and Water layers, the forest topo PDFs, water and treatment, the truck loaded per the Truck load checklist.
