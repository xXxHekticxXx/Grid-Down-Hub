## When this applies
Coming back to the property after evacuating for a storm or fire.

## Do this
1. **Wait for the all-clear** (AlertLake, local radio); roads may have downed lines and trees.
2. **Bring:** water, food, the chainsaw, tools, gloves, boots, flashlight, first aid kit, camera.
3. **Walk the property first:** downed lines (stay 35+ feet away), damaged trees, the camper's shape, standing water, snakes and wildlife displaced by water.
4. **Smell for propane;** shut the tank if you smell gas and air the camper out.
5. **Power:** don't switch on electricity if wiring or the camper got wet; have it checked.
6. **Water:** if the well flooded, see [Well water after a flood](#/a/well-after-flood).
7. **Food:** throw out anything that warmed or got wet ([Fridge and freezer](#/a/fridge-freezer-food-safety)).
8. **Photograph damage** before cleanup; call insurance.
9. **Mold:** dry within 48 hours or remove ([Mold cleanup](#/a/mold-cleanup)).

## Warning signs: get help
- Headache and dizziness inside: CO from a generator or appliance ([Carbon monoxide poisoning](#/a/carbon-monoxide-poisoning)).

## Do NOT
- Do not enter a structure that looks unsafe.
- Do not drive through standing water on the way back.

## What you need
- Cleanup gear, the camera, the Hurricane checklist's "after" section.
