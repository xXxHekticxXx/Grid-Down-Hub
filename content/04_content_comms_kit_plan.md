## NOAA Weather Radio: your grid-down news source
*Sources: [NWS Melbourne office NWR page](https://www.weather.gov/mlb/nwr) · [NWR brochure](https://www.weather.gov/media/nwr/NWR_Brochure_250428.pdf)*

- Broadcasts 24/7 from the National Weather Service on 7 VHF frequencies, **162.400–162.550 MHz**. A basic weather-band radio receives it with no internet or cell service.
- Messages repeat every 4–6 minutes. Range is about **40 miles** from the transmitter.
- **Stations near Orlando** (NWS Melbourne area):

| Station | Location | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **KIH63** | **Orlando** | **162.475 MHz** |
| KIH26 | Daytona Beach | 162.400 MHz |
| WXJ70 | Melbourne | 162.550 MHz |
| WWF69 | Fort Pierce | 162.425 MHz |

## Walkie-talkies: FRS vs. GMRS
*Source: [FCC Personal Radio Services](https://www.fcc.gov/consumers/guides/personal-radio-services-prs-keeping-touch) · [47 CFR 95 Subpart E](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-95/subpart-E)*

| | FRS | GMRS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| License | **None needed** | **FCC license required** (fee; no exam; lasts 10 years; covers immediate family) |
| Typical range | Under 1/2 mile on 0.5 W channels; up to about 2 miles on 2 W channels (depends on terrain) | Farther; handhelds up to 5 W on some channels; mobile and base stations up to 50 W; repeaters extend range |
| Channels | Shared with GMRS channels | 30 channels (16 main + 14 interstitial) |
| Emergency use | Allowed | **Any GMRS channel may be used for emergencies,** and emergency traffic has priority |

## Supply kit checklist (Ready.gov)
*Source: [Ready.gov Build a Kit](https://www.ready.gov/kit)*

Build as tappable checklists, with the checked state saved on-device.

**Basic kit:**
- Water: 1 gallon per person per day for several days
- Food: at least a several-day supply of non-perishables
- Battery or hand-crank radio, **plus NOAA Weather Radio with tone alert**
- Flashlight and extra batteries
- First aid kit
- Whistle (to signal for help)
- Dust mask
- Plastic sheeting, scissors, duct tape
- Moist towelettes, garbage bags, plastic ties (sanitation)
- Wrench or pliers (to turn off utilities)
- Manual can opener
- **Local maps** (paper backup to the app)
- Cell phone with chargers and a **backup battery**

**Add as needed:**
- Soap, hand sanitizer, disinfecting wipes
- Prescription medications, glasses
- OTC meds (pain relievers, anti-diarrhea, antacids)
- **Pet food and extra water for the dog**
- Cash
- Copies of important documents (waterproof container or saved electronically)
- Sleeping bag or blanket, change of clothes, sturdy shoes
- Fire extinguisher, matches in a waterproof container, hygiene supplies

**Kit upkeep:** replace expired items and re-check every year (Ready.gov). App: a yearly reminder each June 1, the start of hurricane season.
