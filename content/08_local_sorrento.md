## Local wildlife: Florida black bears
*Source: [FWC Bear Encounters](https://myfwc.com/wildlifehabitats/wildlife/bear/living/encounter/) · [FWC FAQs](https://myfwc.com/wildlifehabitats/wildlife/bear/living/faqs/)*

Sorrento and Mount Plymouth sit next to the Wekiva area, which has bears. That matters more in a grid-down situation, when trash and food pile up outside.

- Bear seen from a distance: don't approach it.
- **At close range:** stay standing upright, speak calmly and firmly, back away slowly toward a secure area, leave the bear an escape route, and avoid eye contact.
- **Do not run** (bears sprint up to 35 mph), **do not play dead**, and **do not climb a tree** (they climb 100 ft in 30 seconds).
- In your yard: once you are safe, make noise or bang pots to scare it off.
- Huffing, jaw clacking or a bluff charge is a warning, not an attack. Keep backing away.
- **If a black bear attacks: fight back aggressively.**
- Secure trash, pet food and food scraps. Feeding bears is illegal. Report threatening bears to FWC.
